⚙ Manage Lists
How this works
Archive hides a name from new entries but keeps every old record and report unchanged.
Delete is only offered for names that were never used in any record.
A name that is already used in records cannot be renamed — this protects your history.
Share weight applies to attendance you record from now on. To apply a new weight to an older attendance record, open it with EDIT in Shareholder Attendance and tick “re-apply today's weights”.

🩺 Data Health Check
Looks through your records for mistakes that would give wrong shares or wrong ledgers — especially useful when entries arrive automatically. It only reads your data; nothing is changed. Tap a button on any item to jump straight to the record.
📈 Compare Two Periods
Put two periods side by side to see why one share was higher or lower. The numbers use the same calculation as Share Calculation. You can then send the crew a ready message in English, Hinglish or Urdu.

🔒 Locked Periods
After you have paid a period, lock it. Nothing dated inside it can then be added, changed or deleted by accident. If the records ever change some other way (an automatic import, an old backup restored), the lock turns red and shows exactly what changed. The settled figures stay on record as proof of what was paid.
